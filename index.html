I make software that reacts to what you're doing.

The song you're playing, the sentence you just wrote, the agent you just gave a job. Most of what I build lives right on the desktop: Rimlight lights up the edges of your screen with your music, and Undertone puts the lyrics on it, word by word.

Lately I've been building tools for working alongside AI agents, and when I'm not doing that I'm writing a match simulator for FRC robotics. I care about the small stuff: colors pulled from the album art, motion that lands on the beat, and apps that keep your data on your own machine.
